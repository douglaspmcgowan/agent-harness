Research outputs should be essays with a point of view, not bullet-point catalogs.

**Why:** Catalogs are low-value. Synthesis — spotting patterns, tensions, and implications — is what's hard and useful.

**How to apply:** When writing research summaries, identify 2–3 central tensions or themes and structure around those. No flat lists of "paper X says Y."
