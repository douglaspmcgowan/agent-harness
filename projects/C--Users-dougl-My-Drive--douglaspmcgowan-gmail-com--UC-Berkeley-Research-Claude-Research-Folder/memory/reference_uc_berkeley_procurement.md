UC Berkeley research purchasing runs through **BearBuy**. Reach for these rules whenever Doug asks about buying hardware/software on lab/grant funds.

**Dollar thresholds (2025–2026):**

- **Under $10,000, federally funded:** department can self-approve in BearBuy; no competitive bid required.
- **$10,000–$99,999, federal:** competitive bid required — minimum 3 quotes (2 CFR §200.320); routes to an SCM Buyer.
- **$100,000+:** formal competitive bidding (RFx) regardless of funding; IST reviews tech acquisitions ≥$100K before PO.
- **$5,000+ per unit:** capitalization threshold — item must be inventoried/tracked as university property (grant-accounting overhead). Staying just under $5K/unit avoids this.

**Preferred vendors:** Computers = **Dell + Apple** (Dell has a BearBuy _punchout_ catalog for workstations — fastest compliant path). Servers = Dell + HP. Lenovo/HP workstations go through **CDW-G** (also a contracted supplier). Buying outside preferred vendors is allowed but needs a Non-Catalog Form + SCM Buyer justification.

**DIY / used:** A self-assembled PC is allowed but operationally painful (Non-Catalog Form, buyer review, no warranty) — not recommended. **Used/refurbished GPUs have no institutional path** for a grant purchase; effectively off the table.

**How to apply:** Default hardware recommendations to the BearBuy/Dell punchout path. Flag the $10K (competitive-bid) and $5K (capitalization) thresholds when relevant. Source: supplychain.berkeley.edu.
