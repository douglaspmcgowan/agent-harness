On research / knowledge-map pages, synthesize sources into a short essay per topic (or "school"), with inline links to the sources. Do NOT default to one card per article.

**Why:** User told me "they are not all worth their own card" while building ai-schools-of-thought-explorer. Treating every article as an equal card dilutes the signal and makes the site read like a bookmark dump rather than a map of ideas.

**How to apply:**

- Reserve full voice/article cards for canonical, thesis-strong pieces (clear author, clear argument, load-bearing for the school).
- Pool the rest into a "Further reading" list with one-line descriptions.
- The school-level synthesis paragraph should cite 3–6 sources inline, not list them.
- Judge case by case: a school with one defining essay (Sidu on "no product") can have one card; a school built from 10 TLDR-style items should have zero cards and a reading list.
