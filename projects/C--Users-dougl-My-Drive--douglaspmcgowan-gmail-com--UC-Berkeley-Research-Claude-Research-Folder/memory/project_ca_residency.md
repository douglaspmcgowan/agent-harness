Doug is establishing **California residency for tuition purposes** at UC Berkeley to qualify for in-state graduate tuition. He's a US citizen who moved from Pennsylvania (Penn State undergrad), so this is the standard domestic out-of-state → CA path (eligible immigration status is not a blocker).

**Why:** In-state vs. nonresident tuition is a large cost difference for his master's/PhD at Berkeley; ties to the FAFSA + "start on master's stuff" thread. Tracked in the vault at `Obsidian/Metropolis Pt. 1.../20_Personal/Imported Lists/Tasks.md` (open items: "next step with CA residency", "Renew drivers license" due 2026-06-18, "DL test", "Car", "start fafsa").

**How to apply:** UC residency = **physical presence** (continuous 366 days in CA before the residence determination date = first day of instruction, max 6 weeks absent) **+ intent to remain** (CA driver's license, CA voter registration, CA vehicle registration, CA resident tax return, relinquish prior-state ties) **+ file the Statement of Legal Residence (SLR)** with the Office of the Registrar by the term deadline. Date every intent tie as early as possible — late-dated ties weaken the petition even if obtained before the deadline. Status as of 2026-06: getting CA driver's license; voter registration + CA vehicle registration ("Car" task) are the immediate next intent ties.
