A good presentation system does three things for you

The problem isn't making a beautiful slide — it's having to redo them every time.

  • ① Unified Design Language

    Every page shares the same set of tokens— font sizes, colors, and shadows stay consistent.

  • ② Lower Reuse Cost

    Next time on a similar topic, copy the deck, swap the data, switch the theme — done.

  • ③ Switchable & Presentable

    Press T to cycle through themes, press A toggle animations, and impress your audience.
